How to play

  • Roll; the active group's token moves one square at a time. Groups take turns A → B → C → D.
  • Ladders climb; snakes slide down.
  • Red: History · Blue: Geography
  • Green: Political science · Yellow: Economics
  • Landing on a tile shows its question subject.
  • Adjust scores manually with −1, +1, or +5. Reach 40 exactly to win.
